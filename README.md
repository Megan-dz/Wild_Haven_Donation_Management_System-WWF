# 🌿 About Wild Haven

**Wild Haven** is an interactive wildlife conservation and donation platform designed to connect people with conservation initiatives across India.

The platform combines:

🐅 Wildlife awareness  
🌳 Habitat conservation  
📊 Conservation impact tracking  
💚 Donation management  
💳 Digital donation experience  

The objective is simple:

> **Turn wildlife awareness into meaningful conservation action.**

Instead of simply asking visitors to donate, Wild Haven helps them understand **what they are supporting, why it matters, and how their contribution can create an impact.**

---

# 🔄 How Wild Haven Works

The website guides visitors through a simple conservation journey:

```text
        🐾 DISCOVER
             ↓
        🌿 UNDERSTAND
             ↓
        📊 SEE IMPACT
             ↓
        💚 CHOOSE A CAUSE
             ↓
        💰 SELECT DONATION
             ↓
        💳 DEMO PAYMENT
```

Each stage is designed to make the donation experience simple, informative and transparent.

# 5. 📊 See Your Impact

One of the main goals of Wild Haven is to show visitors **what their donation could support**.

Instead of presenting a generic donation request, different contribution levels are connected to conservation activities.

| Contribution | Conservation Initiative |
|---:|---|
| 🐆 **₹1,000** | Guard a Snow Leopard |
| 🐘 **₹5,000** | Rescue an Elephant |
| 🌳 **₹25,000** | Restore One Acre |

---

### 🐆 Guard a Snow Leopard

Supports high-altitude conservation patrols.

### 🐘 Rescue an Elephant

Supports emergency veterinary assistance and relocation.

### 🌳 Restore One Acre

Supports restoration of degraded habitats through native tree planting.

---

Each initiative can lead visitors to a **dedicated conservation campaign page**.

This allows visitors to understand the project before deciding to contribute.

# 🎯 Project Objective

Wild Haven demonstrates how technology can be used to create a more engaging conservation donation experience.

Instead of:

```text
Visitor → Donate
```

Wild Haven creates:

```text
Visitor
   ↓
Understand the Problem
   ↓
Explore Conservation Work
   ↓
See Potential Impact
   ↓
Choose a Cause
   ↓
Donate
```

This helps transform the donation process into an **educational and interactive experience**.

# ⚠️ Demo Disclaimer

> **Wild Haven is a demonstration/prototype project.**

The donation and checkout systems demonstrate the user experience of an online conservation platform.

**No real payment is processed.**

Wildlife statistics, campaign funding figures and organisational information displayed in the prototype should be treated as **demonstration content** rather than live NGO reporting.
